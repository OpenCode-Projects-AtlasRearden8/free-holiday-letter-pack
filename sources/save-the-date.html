Swap in your occasion

  • Wedding — keep as printed.
  • Winter party — change ink colors to cranberry & gold, title to "Save the Snow Date."
  • Birthday / graduation / reunion — same card, kinder words, cake guaranteed.
  • Baby's first day — title "Save the Cuddle Date," no rsvp necessary.

How to use this page

1. Change any name, date, or place in the HTML file — the placeholders are examples.

2. Run python3 build.py --only save-the-date to rebuild the PDF.

3. Print on cardstock for a poster-style announcement, or fold a corner crease and mail it as-is in a 9x6 envelope.

Good news travels; terrible puns travel faster.
