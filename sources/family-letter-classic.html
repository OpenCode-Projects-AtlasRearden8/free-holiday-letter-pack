Dear friends, another December finds us older, hopefully wiser, and definitely noisier. We welcome you to the annual report of the Henderson household, summarized for your reading comfort.

The year opened gently: one new job, one new bicycle, one stubborn daffodil that arrived in February like an early guest. By summer, the porch swing finally had its cushion, and the family road trip made it past four states without a single argument worth remembering.

In autumn, we did what we do best: cooking far too much food and inviting anyone too polite to run. The kids are taller, the dog is rounder, and the cat maintains her longstanding policy of ignoring all of us, then demanding chairs.

You remain in our holiday greetings list not by tradition alone, but because you have been kind to us in the plain, steady way that matters. May your season be warm and your new year be generous.

"Joy shared is joy doubled; dessert shared is dessert halved, so we will share joy instead."
Alex, Sam & Milo
and Buttercup, honorary correspondent
