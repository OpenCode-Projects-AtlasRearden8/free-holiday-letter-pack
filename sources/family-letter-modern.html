Our year in the shortest letter we could manage

Dear friends and family, it has been a full, good year. We moved gardens around, lost a glove, found a better recipe, and finally fixed the porch light. The kids grew more than the tomatoes, which is saying something, because the tomatoes did great.

In March we taught Milo to ride without training wheels, in July we took the trip Grandma has been planning since forever, and in October our street's block party had actual traffic cones again. There were ordinary Tuesdays too, and honestly we loved most of them.

Wherever you are this season, we hope your table is full, your batteries are charged, and your socks have no holes. Come see us if you can. We mean it.

Alex, Sam & Milo
